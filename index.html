Unzips in-browser (pure-JS DEFLATE), auto-pairs every .assets file with its .resource/.resS companions, then parses Unity SerializedFile AudioClip/Texture2D/Mesh records and FSB5 sample banks: Vorbis/PCM/MP3 audio, Alpha8/RGB24/RGBA32/DXT1/DXT5 textures, and uncompressed meshes (OBJ export). Sprites/UI-sized textures sort into Graphics, block-compressed ones into Image. Other compressed formats (BC7/ASTC/ETC) and skinned/BlendShape meshes still export as raw bytes. Downloads tab collects everything in one place. No files leave your browser.
